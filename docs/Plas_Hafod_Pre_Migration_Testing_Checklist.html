3. End-to-End Email Lifecycle Testing

Conduct a complete dry-run of a reservation to verify all 5 stages of communication:

Step A: Initial Inquiry Dispatch

  • Host Alert: Check NOTIFICATION_EMAIL inbox (bob@clic.cymru). Confirm receipt of the dark-themed dashboard alert with complete booking breakdown, guest notes, and direct link to Sanity Studio.
  • Guest Receipt: Check the test guest's email inbox. Confirm receipt of the branded Croeso / Welcome confirmation email with booking summary and host details.

Step B: Deposit Request from Sanity Studio

  • Open Sanity Studio.
  • Navigate to Booking Engine $\rightarrow$ Cottages $\rightarrow$ Bookings.
  • Open the test booking inquiry.
  • Click Send Deposit Request Email.
  • Verify that the guest receives the BACS transfer email with:
    • Deposit amount (25%)
    • Sort Code, Account Number, and Bank Name
    • Unique Payment Reference (e.g. PH-2611-TEST)
    • Correct cottage address in Nebo, Penygroes (LL54 6EB)

Step C: Confirming Booking & Dates Blocking

  • In Sanity Studio, click Mark Paid & Confirmed ✓.
  • Refresh the website calendar on the frontend and confirm the dates are now blocked out to prevent double-booking.

Step D: Final Balance & 14-Day Automation

  • In the booking card in Sanity Studio, review the Check-in & Final Balance Automation Box.
  • Test Unpaid Balance Scenario:
    • Leave Final Balance Paid unchecked.
    • Click Send 14-Day Balance Reminder Now ✉️.
    • Verify the guest receives the Balance Due Reminder with remaining balance amount, BACS details, and notice that the key code will follow once paid.
  • Test Paid Balance & Check-in Guide Scenario:
    • Click ✓ Mark Final Balance as Paid (BACS Received).
    • Click Send Check-in Guide Now 🚀.
    • Verify the guest receives the Arrival Guide email containing:
      • Key safe code (e.g., PH-4821 or custom override)
      • High-speed WiFi SSID and password
      • Driving directions via Penygroes and Nebo
      • Check-in (4:00 PM) & check-out (10:00 AM) times
      • Waste and recycling bin locations

Step E: Automated Cloudflare Cron Trigger

  • Test the cron endpoint via browser or curl:
    curl -s "https://plas-hafod-cottage.bob-83d.workers.dev/api/cron/automated-emails?dryRun=true&secret=plas-hafod-cron-2026"
  • Verify HTTP 200 response with JSON summary:
    • success: true
    • totalEvaluated: [count of active bookings]
    • actionsDispatched: 0 (or list of actions if bookings are within 14 days)

  1. Log in to Cloudflare DNS Dashboard for zone plashafodcottage.co.uk.
  2. Review Existing DNS Records:
    • Note the old LiteSpeed server IP (185.151.30.138).
  3. Configure Worker Custom Domain:

    • Verify immediate 200 OK response from the Cloudflare Worker.
