3. Daily Workflow: Managing Inquiries & Payments

Here is the simple, step-by-step routine for handling guest reservations from initial inquiry through to check-in:

📋 Reservation & Payment Lifecycle Overview
1 Guest requests dates on website → Instant email alert arrives in your inbox.
2 Open Sanity Studio → Click 'Send Deposit Request Email' with 1 click.
3 Guest transfers deposit via BACS → Click 'Mark Paid & Confirmed ✓' (Calendar locks).
4 14 days before arrival → Final balance reminder or key safe arrival pack is dispatched.

Step 1: Receiving a New Booking

When a guest chooses dates on your website and submits an inquiry:

  • You receive an instant email notification summarizing the guest's name, phone, email, dates, party size, dogs, and total price.
  • The inquiry appears under Booking Engine $\rightarrow$ Cottages $\rightarrow$ [Plas Hafod or Bryn Bugeiliaid Bach] $\rightarrow$ Bookings.
  • The status badge will show: NEW INQUIRY.

Step 2: Reviewing the Inquiry

  1. Click on the guest's booking record to open it.
  2. Review their dates, number of guests, and any personal notes in the Guest Notes & Special Requests field.

Step 3: Sending the Deposit Request (1-Click Dispatch)

Right inside the booking card, you will see the Deposit Request & BACS Payment Dispatcher:

  1. (Optional) Add a personal greeting in the Personal Host Message box (e.g., "We'd be delighted to welcome you and your spaniel to Nebo!").
  2. Click the dark button: 📧 Send Deposit Request Email.
  3. The system instantly delivers a professional, branded payment email to the guest with:
    • Your bank name, sort code, and account number.
    • Their exact 25% deposit amount.
    • Their unique payment reference (e.g., PH-2611-JONES).
    • A 48-hour provisional hold notice.
  4. The inquiry status updates to: DEPOSIT SENT.

Step 4: Confirming the Booking (Locking the Calendar)

Once the guest's deposit transfer clears in your bank account:

  1. Open the booking in Sanity.
  2. Click the green button: Mark Paid & Confirmed ✓.
  3. What happens automatically:
    • The status updates to PAID & CONFIRMED.
    • The booking dates are instantly blocked on the website calendar, so no other guest can book those nights.

Step 5: Handling the Final Balance & Check-in Guide

Two weeks (14 days) before the guest's check-in date, the automated system evaluates the booking:

If the Guest Has Transferred Their Remaining Balance:

  1. In the booking card, click: ✓ Mark Final Balance as Paid (BACS Received).
  2. The status updates to BALANCE PAID · KEY SENT.
  3. The automated system (or your 1-click Send Check-in Guide Now 🚀 button) emails the guest their complete arrival pack:
    • Key safe code for their cottage.
    • WiFi network name and password.
    • Step-by-step driving directions from Penygroes to Nebo.
    • Check-in (4:00 PM) and check-out (10:00 AM) times.
    • Bin day / recycling instructions.

If the Remaining Balance Is Still Unpaid:

  • The system will withhold the key safe code for safety.
  • It will automatically email the guest a friendly 14-Day Balance Due Reminder with your BACS details and payment reference.

4. Managing Availability & Blocking Dates

If you need to close dates for maintenance (e.g., chimney sweeping, re-painting), private family visits, or telephone bookings taken outside the website:

  1. In the sidebar, navigate to Booking Engine $\rightarrow$ Cottages $\rightarrow$ [Plas Hafod or Bryn Bugeiliaid Bach] $\rightarrow$ Blocked Dates.
  2. Click + Create in the top right.
  3. Fill in:
    • Start Date: The first date of closure.
    • End Date: The date the property becomes available again.
    • Reason: Select Booked, Maintenance, Owner Stay, or Other.
    • Internal Notes: A quick reminder for yourselves (e.g., "Annual chimney sweep & spring deep clean").
  4. Click the green Publish button at the bottom right.
  5. Those dates will immediately display as blocked and unavailable on the website calendar.
ℹ️ NOTE
To remove a block: Simply open the blocked date entry, click the three dots (...) in the bottom right, and select Delete.

5. Adjusting Pricing & Tariffs

Standard Base Nightly Rates

  1. In the sidebar, go to Booking Engine $\rightarrow$ Cottages $\rightarrow$ [Plas Hafod or Bryn Bugeiliaid Bach] $\rightarrow$ Rates.
  2. Here you can adjust:
    • Base Rate: Standard nightly price outside of special seasons.
    • Deposit Percentage: Default is 25%.
    • Cleaning Fee: Default is £45 for Plas Hafod / £35 for Bryn Bugeiliaid Bach.
  3. Click Publish.

Seasonal Tariffs (Peak Summer, Autumn Gold, Festive Season)

  1. Go to Booking Engine $\rightarrow$ Seasons.
  2. Click on any season to edit its dates or nightly rate:
    • Summer Peak (Haf): e.g., £245/night, 3-night minimum.
    • Autumn Gold (Hydref): e.g., £195/night, 2-night minimum.
    • Festive Season (Nadolig a Blwyddyn Newydd): e.g., £285/night, 4-night minimum.
  3. Click Publish. The website automatically calculates stays that cross across multiple seasons.

Special Offers & Discounts

  1. Go to Booking Engine $\rightarrow$ Special offers / discounts.
  2. You have pre-configured discounts:
    • 7+ Night Full Week Retreat: 12% off.
    • 14+ Night Fortnight Stay: 20% off.
    • Autumn 4-for-3 Special: Book 3 nights, get 4th free.
  3. You can adjust the percentage, minimum nights, or toggle offers on/off with the Active switch.

6. Updating Key Safe Codes, WiFi & Bank Details

Whenever you change physical key safe codes or update bank details, you can update them in one place:

  1. In the sidebar, click Site Settings $\rightarrow$ Site Settings & Branding.
  2. Scroll to the relevant section:
    • 🏦 Bank Transfer & Deposit Details: Update your Account Name, Bank Name, Sort Code, or Account Number.
    • 🔑 Check-in, Key Safe & WiFi Automation:
      • Plas Hafod Cottage: Key safe code, WiFi name, WiFi password.
      • Bryn Bugeiliaid Bach: Key safe code, WiFi name, WiFi password.
      • Arrival Directions: Text instructions for driving up to Nebo.
      • Waste Guidance: Recycling and bin day information.
  3. Click the green Publish button.
  4. All future automated check-in emails will immediately use your updated codes and instructions.

7. Managing Special Offer Banners & Popups

To highlight an offer or seasonal deal on your website:

  1. Open Site Settings $\rightarrow$ Site Settings & Branding.
  2. Open the 🎁 Special Offers & Announcements section:
    • Offer Presentation Style: Choose Top Announcement Banner, Special Offer Popup Modal, Both, or Disabled / None.
    • Banner Text: Edit the text that appears across the top of the screen (e.g. "BOOK DIRECT & SAVE 10% — BEST PRICE GUARANTEED").
    • Popup Details: Edit the heading, terms, and promo badge.
  3. Click Publish. The changes take effect on the live website within seconds.

8. Essential Best Practices & Tips

  • Always click the green "Publish" button: When you make an edit to any page, date, or setting, your change is saved as a draft. Click the green Publish button in the bottom right corner to push it live to visitors.
  • Previewing vs. Live: Yellow marks mean you have uncommitted draft changes. Once published, the status badge turns green.
