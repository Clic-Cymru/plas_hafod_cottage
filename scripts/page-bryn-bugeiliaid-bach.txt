BRYN  BUGEILIAID BACH  

					 A cozy self-contained annexe to the main property offering the same stunning views

									 A beautifully restored 2 bed accommodation overlooking Dyffryn Nantlle and nestled just below the impressive Nantlle Ridge in beautiful Snowdonia. Enjoy unrivalled views of the North Wales coast looking over to Anglesey and beyond from the impressive addition of a well appointed conservatory.
 The property boast a cozy log fire in the main living area, including underfloor heating on the ground floor and central heating on the 1st floor where there are 2 bedrooms with comfortable double beds and spacious shower room.
 Kitchen is well equipped and includes dining area for 4 persons or why not enjoy the great outdoors and relax in the evening by the fire pit in the slated and grassed garden which includes dining area and separate seating area to appreciate the breathtaking views .
 Please Note – Unlike our other property Bryn Bugeiliaid Bach cannot accomodate pets.
